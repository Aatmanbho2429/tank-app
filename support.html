Need help or found a bug? Email aatmanbhoraniya12@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Where is my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone only. TANK works offline and has no account.

How do I log a set?

On Today, tap an exercise, set the weight and reps with the wheels, then tap the check mark.

Why can't I edit yesterday's workout?

Workouts lock after midnight and move to History, so your records stay accurate.

Can I remove the sample split and exercises?

Yes. Delete the "Push Pull Legs (Sample)" split from Splits, and any exercise from Library. They won't come back.

How do I start over?

Profile → Settings → Reset all data. This can't be undone.
