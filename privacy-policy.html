TANK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

TANK ("the app") is a workout tracker made by Aatman Bhoraniya. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect any data

TANK has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your profile: name, gender, weight, height, date of birth, activity level and nutrition goal.
  • Your exercises, photos you add to them, training splits and workout logs.
  • App settings.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It's used only to show your workouts, progress, levels and calorie estimates. It's included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have them turned on, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Photos

If you add a picture to an exercise, you choose it with the system photo picker. TANK only receives the photo you pick and stores a smaller copy on your device. It never sees the rest of your library.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Settings → Reset all data to erase everything, or delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children

TANK doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Calorie and macro targets are general estimates for fitness purposes and are not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
